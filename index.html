How AlgoRecall Works

One connected, adaptive DSA learning system

AlgoRecall coordinates five learning engines into a unified lifecycle: Roadmap â†’ Daily Plan â†’ Problem Solving â†’ Spaced Repetition â†’ Readiness Analytics. Here is how features connect and when to use each:

1. Today's Learning vs 120-Day Roadmap

Daily execution cockpit vs Long-term strategic planner

Today's Learning is your daily cockpit: it filters out the noise and gives you only what you need to solve right now (Learn, Revise, Spaced Recall). The 120-Day Roadmap is your big-picture calendar: use it to inspect upcoming topics, reorder topics for upcoming interviews, and manage buffer days.

2. Practice vs Spaced Revision

Building problem-solving intuition vs Locking memory into long-term recall

Practice is exploratory: use it to discover new algorithmic patterns, browse sheets (Striver, NeetCode), and solve problems at your own pace. Revision is scientific: it schedules previously solved problems at expanding intervals (1d, 3d, 7d, 14d, 30d) so you never forget a technique you already learned.

3. Topic Revision vs Problem-Specific Review

Conceptual breadth drill vs Timed retrieval of a single problem

Topic Revision lets you pick an entire topic (e.g. Binary Trees) and drills multiple questions to rebuild confidence in that area. Problem-Specific Review is driven by Leitner spaced repetition: it tests recall on a specific problem exactly when your memory curve is about to decay.

4. Progress Analytics vs Actionable Recommendations

Diagnostic evidence vs Prescribed immediate next step

Analytics diagnose where you have been: your independent solve rate, time efficiency, and failure categories. Recommendations translate that evidence into immediate action: e.g. "Because you struggled with edge cases on Two Pointers, solve this stepping-stone problem next."

5. Skip vs Reschedule vs Mark Complete

Defer today vs Move to future buffer day vs Record solve & advance SRS

â€¢ Mark Complete / Solved: Records your attempt, updates mastery, and calculates your next spaced repetition date.
â€¢ Skip: Temporarily steps over the task during today's study session (it stays in your queue to finish later today).
â€¢ Reschedule: Moves the task to a future buffer day in your 120-day roadmap without penalizing your streak.
